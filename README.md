## 📱 Content Strategy at a Glance

### Weekly Posting Schedule
- **Monday:** Education (carousel/reel)
- **Tuesday:** Connection (personal content)
- **Wednesday:** Education (tip/tutorial)
- **Thursday:** Engagement (polls/questions)
- **Friday:** Inspiration (client wins/reflection)
- **Weekend:** Casual content/planning

### Your 5 Content Pillars
1. **Education (40%)** - Tips, how-tos, expert insights
2. **Inspiration (20%)** - Success stories, motivation, journey
3. **Connection (20%)** - Personal stories, behind-the-scenes
4. **Engagement (15%)** - Questions, polls, community
5. **Conversion (5%)** - Offers, testimonials, sales

## 🎓 Tools & Resources You'll Need

### Essential Tools (Free/Low-Cost)
- **Email Marketing:** ConvertKit (free up to 1,000), MailerLite, Flodesk
- **Scheduling:** Calendly (free plan), TidyCal
- **Landing Pages:** Carrd ($19/year), Notion (free), Canva
- **Payment:** Stripe (2.9% + 30¢), PayPal
- **Design:** Canva (free or Pro $13/month)
- **Forms:** Google Forms (free), Typeform (free plan)

### Recommended Tools (As You Grow)
- **Course Platform:** Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific
- **CRM:** Dubsado, HoneyBook
- **Contracts:** HelloSign, Dubsado
- **Social Scheduling:** Later, Planoly, Meta Business Suite (free)
- **Community:** Circle, Mighty Networks, Facebook Groups (free)

### Investment Breakdown
**Minimal Launch:** $0-50 (using all free tools)
**Recommended Launch:** $200-300 (Canva Pro, Flodesk, Carrd)
**Premium Setup:** $500+ (Kajabi, Dubsado, etc.)
