## 📝 Content Templates

### Template 1: Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS)

**Hook:** "Struggling to show up consistently on social media? Here's why..."

**Problem:** You have so many ideas but no system. You post when inspired (which isn't often). Your content feels scattered and you're not seeing growth.

**Agitate:** Meanwhile, other founders are showing up daily, building communities, and landing clients. You know you need to be visible, but the overwhelm keeps you stuck.

**Solution:** You don't need more ideas—you need a content system. Here's the 3-step framework I teach my clients: [Share framework]

**CTA:** Save this for later! And DM me "CONTENT" if you want my free content calendar template.

---

### Template 2: Storytelling

**Hook:** "I used to hide behind my business..."

**Story:** When I first started, I was terrified to show my face. I thought my content had to be perfect. I worried about what people would think—especially as a Muslim woman in a visible space.

Then I realized: my story IS my brand. The messy journey, the faith struggles, the real me—that's what people connect with.

**Lesson:** Authenticity beats perfection every time.

**CTA:** If you're hiding, this is your sign to step into the light. Your people are waiting for the real you.

---

### Template 3: Listicle

**Hook:** "5 things I wish I knew before building my personal brand"

1. **Your niche will evolve** - Don't wait for perfect clarity. Start where you are.

2. **Consistency beats perfection** - Show up regularly, even if it feels awkward at first.

3. **Your story is your secret weapon** - Share what makes you different.

4. **Community over followers** - 100 engaged followers > 10K ghost followers.

5. **Rest is part of the strategy** - You can't pour from an empty cup. Schedule rest.

**CTA:** Which one resonates most with you? Comment below! 👇

---

### Template 4: Myth-Busting

**Hook:** "MYTH: You need to be on every platform to grow your brand"

**Truth:** Being everywhere means being nowhere.

Here's what actually works:
- Pick 1-2 platforms where your ideal clients hang out
- Master those before expanding
- Show up consistently
- Engage authentically

I only use Instagram and email marketing. That's it. And I've built a 6-figure business.

You don't need to be everywhere. You need to be strategic.

**CTA:** What platform is working best for you? Let me know below!

## 🕌 Ramadan Content Strategy

### Content Adjustments for Ramadan

**Reduce Frequency:**
- Post 3-4x per week instead of daily
- Batch create before Ramadan starts
- Focus on evergreen content

**Content Themes:**
- Business lessons from Ramadan
- Productivity while fasting
- Spiritual entrepreneurship
- Giving back and charity
- Reflection and intention setting
- Ramadan routine + business

**Posting Times:**
- Early morning (before Fajr)
- After Iftar
- Late evening (after Taraweeh)

**Respectful Boundaries:**
- Less sales content
- More value and inspiration
- Gentle CTAs
- Focus on community and connection

## 🎨 Content Batching System

### Monthly Batching Routine

**Week 1: Planning**
- Choose content themes for the month
- Brainstorm post ideas
- Plan out captions
- Source or create graphics
- Schedule filming day

**Week 2: Creation**
- Film all reels in one day (2-3 hours)
- Take photos for posts (1 hour)
- Design graphics in Canva (2 hours)
- Write all captions (2 hours)

**Week 3: Scheduling**
- Upload to scheduler (Later, Planoly, Meta Business Suite)
- Schedule out entire month
- Prep stories content
- Plan engagement times

**Week 4: Engagement**
- Focus on DMs and comments
- Story interactions
- Build relationships
- Monitor performance
- Adjust next month's strategy
