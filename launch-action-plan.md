# Launch Action Plan
## 8-Week Roadmap to Launch Your First Offer

---

## 🎯 Which Offer Should You Launch First?

### Start with: **Digital Product** OR **6-Week Mentorship**

**Why Digital Product?**
✅ One-time creation, infinite sales
✅ Lower price point (easier yes)
✅ Builds email list
✅ Creates passive income
✅ Scales without your time

**Why 6-Week Mentorship?**
✅ Higher price point (faster revenue)
✅ Test your curriculum live
✅ Gather testimonials
✅ Build case studies
✅ Learn what clients need most

**My Recommendation:** Launch 6-week mentorship first (with 3-5 founding members), then turn it into your digital product!

## 📅 8-Week Launch Plan

### Week 1-2: Planning & Strategy

#### Week 1: Foundations
**Day 1-2: Define Your Offer**
- [ ] Choose your offer (6-week mentorship recommended)
- [ ] Name your program
- [ ] Write your offer description
- [ ] Define outcomes/transformation
- [ ] List what's included
- [ ] Set your price ($1,500-2,200 suggested)

**Day 3-4: Identify Your Client**
- [ ] Define ideal client avatar
- [ ] List their pain points (what keeps them up at night?)
- [ ] Identify their desires (what do they dream about?)
- [ ] Map their objections (what stops them from buying?)
- [ ] Write their "before" and "after" state

**Day 5-7: Build Your Curriculum**
- [ ] Outline 6 weeks of content (use framework provided)
- [ ] Create weekly themes
- [ ] List deliverables for each week
- [ ] Prepare workbooks/templates
- [ ] Plan homework assignments

#### Week 2: Sales Assets
**Day 8-10: Create Sales Page**
- [ ] Write compelling headline
- [ ] Draft sales copy (see template below)
- [ ] Include testimonials (or beta invite)
- [ ] Add FAQ section
- [ ] Create payment options
- [ ] Design page (Canva, WordPress, Carrd, Notion)

**Day 11-12: Application & Forms**
- [ ] Create discovery call application
- [ ] Set up Calendly/booking system
- [ ] Draft discovery call script
- [ ] Create client agreement/contract
- [ ] Set up payment processor (Stripe, PayPal, ThriveCart)

**Day 13-14: Lead Magnet**
- [ ] Choose lead magnet type (workbook, checklist, challenge)
- [ ] Create the freebie
- [ ] Design in Canva
- [ ] Set up email delivery (ConvertKit, MailerLite)
- [ ] Write welcome sequence (5 emails)

---

### Week 3-4: Content Creation

#### Week 3: Build Anticipation
**Day 15-17: Awareness Content**
- [ ] Post about your journey (why you're creating this)
- [ ] Share transformation stories
- [ ] Educate on the problem you solve
- [ ] Tease the solution (don't reveal yet)
- [ ] Build curiosity

**Example Posts:**
- "I'm creating something special for Muslim women founders..."
- "The #1 mistake I see founders make with their brand"
- "Why I decided to launch a mentorship program"
- "The transformation I want to help you achieve"

**Day 18-21: Value Bombs**
- [ ] Post daily value content
- [ ] Share tips from your program
- [ ] Do Instagram Lives or Stories Q&As
- [ ] Create carousels/reels
- [ ] Get people engaging

**Content Ideas:**
- Free mini-training
- Case study/success story
- Behind-the-scenes of program creation
- "What you'll learn in my program" teaser
- Poll: "What's your biggest brand challenge?"

#### Week 4: Email List Building
**Day 22-24: Promote Lead Magnet**
- [ ] Announce your freebie
- [ ] Post about it 3-5x across platforms
- [ ] Run Instagram Stories campaign
- [ ] Send to current email list (if you have one)
- [ ] Collaborate with others to share

**Day 25-28: Nurture Sequence Kicks In**
- [ ] Monitor email sign-ups
- [ ] Engage with everyone who downloads
- [ ] Reply to email responses
- [ ] Share results: "X people downloaded!"
- [ ] Keep building anticipation

---

### Week 5-6: Pre-Launch

#### Week 5: The Waitlist
**Day 29-31: Open Waitlist**
- [ ] Announce waitlist opening
- [ ] Create simple waitlist form (Google Forms, Typeform)
- [ ] Share waitlist benefits (early access, founding member pricing)
- [ ] Post daily about the program
- [ ] Share curriculum sneak peeks

**Waitlist Post Ideas:**
- "Doors open [DATE] but you can join the waitlist now"
- "Founding members get [bonus/discount]"
- "Only X spots available for this round"
- "Swipe to see what's inside the program"

**Day 32-35: Social Proof & Urgency**
- [ ] Share why you created this
- [ ] Address objections in content
- [ ] Show behind-the-scenes prep
- [ ] Build trust and credibility
- [ ] Create FOMO (limited spots)

#### Week 6: Pre-Launch Content Blitz
**Day 36-38: FAQs & Objections**
- [ ] Answer common questions via content
- [ ] Address "I don't have time"
- [ ] Address "I can't afford it"
- [ ] Address "I'm not ready"
- [ ] Share payment plans available

**Day 39-42: Final Push**
- [ ] Go Live to explain the program
- [ ] Share testimonials (or your own transformation)
- [ ] Create urgency (limited spots, deadline)
- [ ] Invite people to book discovery calls
- [ ] DM people on waitlist personally

## 💰 Pricing Psychology

### Founding Member Pricing Strategy

**Round 1:** $1,500-1,997 (5 clients max)
- Get testimonials
- Refine curriculum
- Build case studies
- Learn what works

**Round 2:** $2,500-2,997 (10 clients max)
- You have proof it works
- Better materials
- More confidence
- Social proof

**Round 3+:** $3,500-4,997 (Unlimited)
- Established program
- Multiple success stories
- Refined systems
- Premium positioning

### Payment Plans
Always offer payment plans! They increase conversions by 30-50%.

**Example:**
- Full pay: $1,997 (no discount or 10% off)
- 2 payments: $1,047 each ($2,094 total)
- 3 payments: $749 each ($2,247 total)

## ✨ Launch Success Tips

1. **Start building your audience NOW** - Don't wait until launch
2. **Share your process** - Let people see you creating the program
3. **Lead with value** - Teach, don't just sell
4. **Be authentic** - Your story is your selling point
5. **Follow up** - Most sales happen in the DMs
6. **Create urgency** - Limited spots + deadline = action
7. **Celebrate small wins** - Every application is progress
8. **Don't give up** - First launch might be small, that's okay
9. **Over-deliver** - Make your founding members LOVE you
10. **Ask for help** - Share, collaborate, promote each other
