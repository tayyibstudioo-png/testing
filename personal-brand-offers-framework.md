## 🚀 Onboarding Process

### Pre-Enrollment
1. **Discovery Call (30 min - Free)**
   - Understand their goals and challenges
   - Share your program overview
   - Determine if they're a good fit
   - Answer questions

2. **Application Form**
   - Current business stage
   - Main challenges
   - Goals for next 6-12 months
   - Commitment level assessment
   - Budget confirmation

3. **Enrollment**
   - Send contract/agreement
   - Payment options (full pay or payment plan)
   - Welcome email with next steps

### Day 1 Onboarding
1. **Welcome Package**
   - Welcome video from you
   - Program timeline and structure
   - Access to portal/course platform
   - Workbooks and resources
   - Community access link
   - First assignment (pre-work)

2. **Onboarding Call (Week 1)**
   - Get to know each other
   - Review their application and goals
   - Set specific milestones
   - Explain program structure
   - Answer questions
   - Assign homework for next session

### Weekly Rhythm
- **Monday:** Weekly lesson release (for digital product)
- **Mid-week:** 1:1 coaching call (scheduled at their convenience)
- **Friday:** Check-in via Voxer/WhatsApp
- **Sunday:** Weekly wins celebration + accountability

### 1. Awareness Stage (Social Media)
- Share value-driven content
- Tell your story and transformation
- Feature client success stories
- Go live with Q&As
- Post educational carousels/reels

### 2. Consideration Stage (Lead Magnet)
**Free Lead Magnets to Create:**
- "5-Day Brand Clarity Challenge"
- "Muslim Founder's Content Template Pack"
- "Brand Story Framework Workbook"
- "30 Reel Ideas for Service Providers"

### 3. Conversion Stage (Nurture + Sales)
- Email nurture sequence (5-7 emails)
- Invitation to discovery call
- Clear call-to-action
- Testimonials and case studies
- Limited spots or enrollment windows

### 4. Discovery Call Script
**Opening (5 min):**
- Build rapport
- Ask about their journey

**Deep Dive (15 min):**
- What's working/not working?
- What have they tried before?
- What's their biggest frustration?
- Where do they want to be in 12 months?

**Present Solution (5 min):**
- Share how your program solves their problem
- Explain structure and outcomes
- Share success stories

**Close (5 min):**
- Answer objections
- Present investment options
- Ask for the sale
- Next steps

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with 1:1 before scaling** - Test your curriculum with individual clients first
2. **Over-deliver initially** - Build case studies and testimonials
3. **Batch create content** - Record multiple modules in one day
4. **Survey clients constantly** - Improve based on feedback
5. **Create in public** - Share your journey building the offers
6. **Price for your worth** - Don't undercharge; it affects client commitment
7. **Set boundaries early** - Be clear about response times and availability
8. **Celebrate wins** - Share client transformations (with permission)
