## 📞 Discovery Call Script

### Opening (5 minutes)

"Assalamu Alaikum [Name]! It's so nice to finally talk to you. How are you?"

*Small talk, build rapport*

"So I read through your application and I'm really excited to learn more about you and your business. I want to make sure this call is valuable for you, so here's how we'll spend our time:

First, I want to hear more about where you are now and where you want to go. Then I'll share more about how my program works and how I think I can help you. At the end, we'll discuss next steps if it feels like a good fit. Sound good?"

### Discovery Questions (15 minutes)

**Current Situation:**
- "Tell me about your business. What do you do?"
- "How long have you been doing this?"
- "What made you start this business?"

**Challenges:**
- "What's the biggest challenge you're facing right now with your brand?"
- "What have you already tried to fix this?"
- "What's stopping you from reaching your goals?"

**Goals:**
- "Where do you want to be 6 months from now?"
- "What would success look like for you?"
- "What would change in your life if you achieved this?"

**Emotional Hook:**
- "How does it feel right now, dealing with [their challenge]?"
- "What happens if nothing changes?"

### Present Solution (5 minutes)

"Thank you for sharing all of that with me. Based on what you've told me, I really think I can help you. Let me tell you about [Program Name]...

*Explain program structure*

The reason this works is because [tie to their specific challenges].

[Share quick success story/testimonial]

By the end of the program, you'll have [list specific outcomes they mentioned wanting]."

### Handle Objections (5 minutes)

**"I need to think about it"**
"I totally understand! What specifically do you need to think about? Is it the investment, the time commitment, or something else?"

**"I need to talk to my spouse"**
"Of course! What do you think their concerns might be? Let's talk through them now so you're prepared."

**"I'm not sure I have the time"**
"I hear you! Time is tight for all of us. The program is designed for busy founders - it's about 5-7 hours per week. How much time are you currently spending trying to figure this out on your own?"

**"It's too expensive"**
"I understand that it's an investment. Let me ask you - how much is it costing you every month that you're NOT getting clients consistently? What would change if you had [their goal]?"

### Close (3 minutes)

**If they're ready:**
"Amazing! I'm so excited to work with you. Here's what happens next:

1. I'll send you the contract and payment link
2. Once you're enrolled, you'll get instant access to the portal
3. We'll schedule your first call
4. You'll join the private community

Do you want to do the full payment or payment plan?"

**If they're not ready:**
"No pressure at all! Why don't you take [24-48 hours] to think about it and let me know? I'll send you a recap email with all the details. Do you have any other questions I can answer?"

### Follow-Up

**Same day:** Send recap email with details
**Day 3:** Check-in if no response
**Day 7:** Final follow-up, offer alternative (smaller package, waitlist, etc.)

## 📝 Contract Essentials

### Include These Sections:

**1. Program Details**
- Program name
- Duration (start and end date)
- What's included
- Deliverables

**2. Investment & Payment**
- Total investment
- Payment schedule
- Refund policy
- Late payment terms

**3. Expectations**
- Client responsibilities
- Coach responsibilities
- Response time commitments
- Cancellation policy for calls

**4. Boundaries**
- Communication hours
- Response time (24-48 hours)
- Emergency contact policy
- Respect for prayer/family time

**5. Confidentiality**
- You won't share their info
- They can choose whether to be featured

**6. Testimonials & Results**
- Permission to use testimonials
- Permission to share results (anonymously or with attribution)

**7. Termination**
- How either party can end the agreement
- What happens to payments
- Access to materials after termination

**8. Liability**
- You're providing education, not guaranteeing results
- They're responsible for implementing
- Standard liability waiver

**9. Signatures**
- Digital signatures
- Date
- Agreement confirmation

## 💬 Voxer/WhatsApp Support Guidelines

**Set These Boundaries:**

✅ **I'm available for:**
- Quick questions about lessons
- Feedback on content/captions
- Celebrating your wins
- Support during launches
- Troubleshooting tech issues

⏰ **Response Time:**
- I respond within 24 hours on weekdays
- I'm offline Fridays-Sundays (adjust to your schedule)
- For urgent matters, mark message as "urgent"

❌ **Not for:**
- Coaching calls (book a session)
- Long strategy discussions (save for our calls)
- After hours emergencies (nothing is that urgent!)

**Sample Auto-Response:**
"Assalamu Alaikum! I've received your message and will respond within 24 hours. If this is urgent, please mark it as such. Otherwise, I'll get back to you soon, insha'Allah! 🌙"
